# Research Gap Analysis

> **Module**: UX/UI Design & Research | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Identification of technical gaps in current academic and commercial solutions.

## Identified Gaps

1. **Lack of Microclimate Context**: Most CV models ignore ambient humidity and temperature when evaluating infection likelihood.
2. **Extreme Edge Latency**: Cloud-dependent tools fail in real-world farming environments with poor cellular coverage.
3. **Absence of Actionable RAG Advisory**: Existing apps only name the disease without providing safe, contextualized treatment recipes.
