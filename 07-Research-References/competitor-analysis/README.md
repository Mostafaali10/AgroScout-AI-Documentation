# Commercial Competitor & State-of-the-Art Analysis

> **Module**: UX/UI Design & Research | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Comparative benchmark of existing precision agriculture scouting tools.

## Landscape Analysis

| Competitor | Strengths | Limitations | AgroScout Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plantix** | Large community database | Cloud-only, no autonomous rover | Offline-first, autonomous rover, IoT fusion |
| **Taranis** | High-resolution aerial imagery | Expensive subscription, slow turnaround | Real-time edge inference, sub-minute alerts |
| **Agrio** | Multi-crop models | Clunky UI, no integrated hardware | Native cross-platform Flutter app & custom rover |
