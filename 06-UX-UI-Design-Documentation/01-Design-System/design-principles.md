# Agricultural UX Design Principles

> **Module**: UX/UI Design & Research | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Foundational human-interface principles tailored for harsh, outdoor field environments.

## Core Principles

1. **Glanceability Under Direct Sunlight**: High-contrast typography and vivid status badges readable under intense sunlight.
2. **Glove-Friendly Interaction**: Minimum touch targets of 48x48dp with ample spacing to prevent accidental taps.
3. **Clear Offline States**: Unambiguous visual indicators showing whether data is live, cached, or queued for sync.
