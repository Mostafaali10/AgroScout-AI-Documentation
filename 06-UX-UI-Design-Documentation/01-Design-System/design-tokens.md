# Design Tokens Specification

> **Module**: UX/UI Design & Research | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Platform-agnostic spacing, elevation, border-radius, and animation tokens.

## Tokens

- Spacing: `spacing-xs: 4px`, `spacing-sm: 8px`, `spacing-md: 16px`, `spacing-lg: 24px`, `spacing-xl: 32px`
- Border Radius: `radius-sm: 6px`, `radius-md: 12px`, `radius-pill: 999px`
- Elevation: Material 3 elevation levels (0dp to 12dp)
