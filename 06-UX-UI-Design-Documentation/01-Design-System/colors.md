# Color Palette & Theming Specification

> **Module**: UX/UI Design & Research | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Semantic color tokens optimized for accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AAA) in dark, light, and outdoor sunlight modes.

## Color Definitions

- **Primary Green (AgroGreen)**: `#2E7D32` (Health & vitality)
- **Secondary Earth (SoilBrown)**: `#5D4037` (Parcels & earth)
- **Alert Red (DiseaseAlert)**: `#D32F2F` (Foliar infection detected)
- **Warning Amber (MoistureLow)**: `#FFA000` (Sensor threshold warning)
- **Neutral Dark (FieldCharcoal)**: `#121212`
- **Neutral Light (SunlightWhite)**: `#FFFFFF`
