# Stakeholder Matrix

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Identification and analysis of key stakeholders in the AgroScout AI ecosystem.

## Stakeholder Breakdown

1. **Farm Owners & Agricultural Investors**: Focus on ROI, yield forecast, and cost reduction.
2. **Agronomists & Plant Pathologists**: Need precise disease classifications, confidence scores, and historical trend analysis.
3. **Field Scouting Technicians**: Require rugged, intuitive, offline-first mobile workflows.
4. **Agricultural Extension Agents**: Rely on regional outbreak heatmaps and community advisory tools.
