# User Personas

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Detailed behavioral personas representing our core user segments.

## Persona 1: Mahmoud - Commercial Agronomist

- **Age**: 38 | **Farm Size**: 450 Hectares (Wheat & Potatoes)
- **Pain Points**: Hard to monitor vast acreage; needs early blight warning before symptoms cover 10% of field.
- **Goals**: Automated alerts, zone-based risk heatmaps, exact spray volume recommendations.

## Persona 2: Sarah - Field Scouting Technician

- **Age**: 26 | **Environment**: Dusty fields, intermittent 3G network
- **Pain Points**: Clunky apps that crash when connection drops; battery drain.
- **Goals**: Fast photo capture, instant on-device diagnosis, automated background sync.
