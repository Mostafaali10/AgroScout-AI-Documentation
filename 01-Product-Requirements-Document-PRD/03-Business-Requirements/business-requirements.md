# Business Requirements

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Commercial and operational objectives that guide product development and monetization.

## Commercial Objectives

- Provide a scalable SaaS tier for commercial agribusinesses and cooperatives.
- Offer an accessible offline-capable community tier for smallholder farmers.
- Lower agrochemical expenditure by at least 25% through targeted hotspot treatment.

## Regulatory & Compliance

- Compliance with local agrochemical application safety regulations.
- Respect for data sovereignty: farm boundary coordinates and yield estimates remain private to the farm owner.
