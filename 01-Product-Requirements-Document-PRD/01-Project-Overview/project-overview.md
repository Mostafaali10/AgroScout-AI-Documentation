# Project Overview

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

High-level vision, core objectives, and mission of the AgroScout AI platform.

## Executive Summary

AgroScout AI is a comprehensive precision agriculture scouting system integrating autonomous edge IoT devices, deep-learning based foliar disease detection, an AI agronomic advisor, and an offline-first Flutter application.

## Vision Statement

Empower modern farming enterprises and smallholders with automated, accurate, and actionable crop health intelligence to minimize yield losses, optimize chemical interventions, and promote sustainable agriculture.

## Strategic Goals

- Reduce average crop scouting time by 70% through automated mission routing.
- Detect foliar diseases with >95% top-1 accuracy at early onset stages.
- Provide real-time microclimate and soil telemetry via resilient IoT sensor fusion.
- Deliver actionable agronomic guidance directly to field workers even in offline environments.
