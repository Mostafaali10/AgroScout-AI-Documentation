# Problem Statement

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Analysis of modern agricultural scouting challenges, economic losses from late diagnosis, and current technological gaps.

## Industry Challenges

1. **Manual Scouting Bottlenecks**: Traditional field inspection is labor-intensive, slow, and covers less than 5% of large farm acreage.
2. **Late Disease Identification**: Foliar pathogens (e.g., Early Blight, Rust, Powdery Mildew) spread exponentially before visual detection by human scouts.
3. **Overuse of Agro-Chemicals**: Lack of precision leads to blanket pesticide spraying, increasing costs and environmental degradation.
4. **Connectivity Constraints**: Rural agricultural zones frequently lack reliable cellular connectivity, breaking cloud-dependent tools.

## Target Impact

AgroScout AI addresses these gaps by shifting inference to the edge and mobile devices, caching geospatial maps, and providing automated scouting rovers.
