# NFR-05: Usability & Accessibility

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Human-computer interaction guidelines for outdoor, high-glare agricultural environments.

## Design Specifications

- High-contrast outdoor mode with sunlight-readable color palettes.
- Large tap targets (minimum 48x48 dp) accessible while wearing farm gloves.
- Full Arabic and English RTL/LTR interface localization.
