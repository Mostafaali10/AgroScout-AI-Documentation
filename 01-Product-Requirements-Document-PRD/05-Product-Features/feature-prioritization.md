# Feature Prioritization (MoSCoW Matrix)

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Strategic prioritization framework for AgroScout AI releases.

## Must Have (MVP)

- Mobile camera disease inference
- Offline scouting storage & sync
- Farm boundary polygon mapping
- Real-time IoT temperature, humidity & soil moisture monitoring

## Should Have

- AI Agronomic Agent (RAG advisory)
- Automated scouting mission waypoint generator
- Multi-crop disease tracking (Tomato, Potato, Wheat, Corn, Grape)

## Could Have

- Multispectral drone imagery fusion
- Soil nutrient (NPK) predictive modeling

## Won't Have (Immediate Scope)

- Autonomous robot hardware manufacturing (we supply edge firmware)
