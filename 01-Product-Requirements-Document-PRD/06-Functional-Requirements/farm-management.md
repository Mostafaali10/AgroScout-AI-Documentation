# FR-02: Farm & Parcel Management

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for defining farms, fields, crop varieties, and planting seasons.

## Requirements

1. Users shall be able to create farms with custom names, total acreage, and geographic regions.
2. Users shall define parcel boundaries by tapping GPS points on an interactive map or walking the boundary.
3. Users shall assign active crops, planting dates, and expected harvest windows to each parcel.
