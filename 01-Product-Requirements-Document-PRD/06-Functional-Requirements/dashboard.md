# FR-03: Dashboard & Executive Overview

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for high-level health indices, urgent alerts, and recent scouting logs.

## Requirements

1. Dashboard shall display overall Farm Health Index (0-100%) calculated from sensor and scouting data.
2. High-priority disease detection alerts shall be pinned to the top of the dashboard.
3. Real-time weather forecast and microclimate trends shall update hourly.
