# FR-11: History & Agronomic Reporting

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for storing audit logs, scouting history, and generating exportable reports.

## Requirements

1. All scouting events, sensor anomalies, and user actions shall be stored immutably.
2. Users shall generate professional PDF scouting reports including photos, GPS maps, and diagnosis notes.
3. System shall support scheduled weekly automated email delivery of farm health summaries.
