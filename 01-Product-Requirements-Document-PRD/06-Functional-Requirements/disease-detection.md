# FR-06: Crop Disease Detection

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for image-based leaf pathogen identification and severity scoring.

## Requirements

1. User shall capture or upload leaf photos in high resolution (minimum 720p).
2. AI model shall identify pathogen class, common name, scientific name, and detection confidence score.
3. System shall calculate disease severity percentage (affected leaf surface area).
4. System shall function completely offline with on-device quantized neural network weights.
