# FR-05: Live Monitoring & Edge Video Stream

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for real-time video and telemetry streaming from field rovers/cameras.

## Requirements

1. System shall support low-latency WebRTC/RTSP video feed streaming from connected rover cameras.
2. Stream shall display an AI bounding box overlay for real-time detected foliage anomalies.
3. Operator shall be able to trigger snapshot diagnosis directly from the live feed.
