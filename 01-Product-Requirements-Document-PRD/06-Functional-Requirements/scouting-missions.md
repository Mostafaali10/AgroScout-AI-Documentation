# FR-10: Scouting Mission Management

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for planning, executing, and tracking human and autonomous scouting rounds.

## Requirements

1. Farm manager shall generate scouting missions with assigned waypoints and scout checklists.
2. Rover missions shall export MAVLink / WGS84 waypoint coordinates for autonomous navigation.
3. Scout app shall track mission completion percentage, elapsed time, and geolocation trail.
