# FR-09: Farm Analytics & Predictive Modeling

> **Module**: AgroScout AI PRD | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Requirements for yield estimation, disease spread forecasting, and spray efficiency.

## Requirements

1. System shall compute weekly disease progression velocity across adjacent parcels.
2. Analytics engine shall correlate high moisture spells with incubation periods of late blight.
3. System shall report historical pesticide reduction metrics over baseline seasons.
