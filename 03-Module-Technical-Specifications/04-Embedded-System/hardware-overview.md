# Embedded System & Scouting Rover Hardware Overview

> **Module**: Technical Specifications | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Autonomous scouting rover, edge camera pod, and field environmental station.

## Hardware Archetypes

1. **Scouting Rover**: All-terrain 4WD robotic platform with Raspberry Pi CM4 and Sony IMX477 camera.
2. **Fixed Solar Station**: ESP32-S3 sensor hub monitoring soil moisture, leaf wetness, and solar radiation.
