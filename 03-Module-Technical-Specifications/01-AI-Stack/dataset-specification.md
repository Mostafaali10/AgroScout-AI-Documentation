# Dataset Specification & Schema

> **Module**: Technical Specifications | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Structure, annotations, and sourcing for training and validation datasets.

## Data Sources

- PlantVillage dataset baseline (54,306 images across 14 crops and 26 diseases).
- In-field proprietary dataset: 25,000 field-captured images under variable sunlight, dust, and shadow conditions.
- Annotation format: YOLO format bounding boxes and COCO segmentation masks.
