# Definition of Ready (DoR)

> **Module**: Project Management & QA | **Status**: Approved Baseline

Checklist a backlog user story must satisfy before entering an active sprint.

## DoR Criteria

1. User story format defined with clear business value.
2. Acceptance criteria written in Gherkin (Given/When/Then).
3. Wireframes attached if user-facing.
4. Story points estimated by the engineering team.
